Cosmo Martians 👽

A shared easter egg for my portfolio in the style of 1950s pulp science fiction — scary first, funny second. Without music the martians gather at the screen edges and get in the way. Press Play test music: the crowd dances, then their heads burst one by one — helmet shards, blood, brains and an eyeball. Sometimes it all hits the screen from inside. The headless body waves goodbye; the eyeball rolls away.

Source code

🐸

Add to any project with one line:

<script src="https://dubnovalexey.github.io/cosmo-martians/martians.js"
        data-project="library" defer></script>
